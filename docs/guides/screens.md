# A tour of the screens

Screenshots of the demo hotel *Lotus Riverside Hotel* (18 rooms in Phnom
Penh), taken on 13 November 2026 with the English interface unless stated.
Guests, staff and bookings are fictitious; they are generated by
`tools/screenshots/` (see its README), which also retakes every picture when the
interface changes.

Each staff member sees only the menu entries of their role. The screenshots
are taken as the super administrator, who sees everything.

## Signing in, languages

![Sign-in page](../images/login.png)

Every person signs in with their own account and chooses their language
(English, Français, 简体中文, Русский, ខ្មែរ); the choice follows them on every
device.

## Dashboard

![Dashboard](../images/dashboard.png)

Tonight's occupancy, arrivals, departures and guests in house, the month's
occupancy, ADR and RevPAR (revenue figures only for those with *Financial
reports*), the next 14 nights on the books, room readiness and inventory.

## Front desk

![Front desk: today's arrivals](../images/front-desk.png)

Arrivals, guests in house, departures and the status of every room for the
chosen day, with each stay's balance. Check-in needs an assigned room; when
the room is not clean yet, the receptionist is told and may check in anyway.
*Walk-in* opens a booking for tonight with the walk-in source.

![Front desk on an iPad](../images/ipad-front-desk.png)

## Calendar

![Room calendar, two weeks](../images/calendar.png)

One line per room, grouped by category with the number of rooms left each
night; bookings without a room yet are on the *To assign* line. Click an empty
day to book; drag a booking to change room or dates (long-press on tablets).
The database refuses any double allocation of a room, whichever device made
the change.

![Calendar on an iPad](../images/ipad-calendar.png)

## Reservations

![Reservations in house](../images/reservations.png)

![A reservation: stay, rate, folio, payments and history](../images/reservation.png)

The reservation page holds the stay (room, dates, rate plan, nightly prices,
cancellation terms), the guest folio (room nights, extras, taxes, payments,
balance), invoices and receipts as PDF, and the complete history of changes.

## Restaurant & bar

![Tables with their open checks](../images/restaurant-floor.png)

![Kitchen display](../images/restaurant-kitchen.png)

Checks per table, takeaway and room service; the kitchen and the bar see
their own items with the time since they were sent. A check can be paid or
charged to the guest's room.

## Vehicle rental

![Rental schedule](../images/vehicles-schedule.png)

Scooters, cars with or without driver, tuk-tuks and bicycles: booking,
hand-over with licence, odometer, fuel and deposit, return, and payment or
charge to the room.

## Pricing & rates

![Rate calendar](../images/pricing-calendar.png)

Rate plans (derived plans, meal plans, cancellation policies), seasons and
pricing rules, and the daily rate calendar with restrictions. One pricing
engine computes every price.

## Channel manager

![SiteMinder connection and code mapping](../images/channels-mapping.png)

Connection to SiteMinder (pmsXchange), mapping of room types and rate plans
to the channel's codes, received bookings and the log of every exchange. Shown
here in the test environment, with sending and receiving switched off.

## End of day

![End of day: open items, the day's figures and cash-up, closed days](../images/night-audit.png)

Run after midnight for the day that ended: guests expected who did not arrive
(mark a no-show), guests due out still in house, open restaurant checks,
vehicles not returned, the next day's arrivals without a room and balances
due; then the day's figures and the cash-up by payment method. Closing records
the day's report (PDF) and freezes the day: charges of that day and payments
received that day can no longer be posted, voided or changed; corrections are
made on the current day.

## Photos and logo

*Configuration → Hotel profile* holds the logo. It appears in the header and
on every document: invoices, receipts, folios, restaurant bills, rental
contracts and day reports. Room categories and facilities have photos, up to
20 each, in the order you choose. Every image is resized when it is uploaded
and loses its location data.

be ended), sign-in history and the audit trail.
